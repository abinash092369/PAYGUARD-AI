## Strategic Relevance

Razorpay is India's leading omnichannel payments and financial services platform, processing tens of billions of dollars in transaction volume annually. Operating at this scale requires state-of-the-art AI risk management infrastructure to protect merchants and customers from sophisticated fraud while ensuring near-zero false positive friction.

PayGuard AI demonstrates direct domain knowledge and engineering capabilities aligned with Razorpay's AI Risk team priorities:

1. **Transaction Risk Scoring & Observability**:
   - Demonstrates real-time feature engineering (velocity scores, geolocation distance, device fingerprinting, account age ratios) critical for merchant risk analysis.

2. **Explainable AI for Risk Operations**:
   - Risk operations teams and merchants need transparent reasons when transactions are flagged or blocked. PayGuard AI provides grounded threat factor badges and dynamic natural-language explanations.

3. **Automated Security Alert Management**:
   - High-volume payment gateways require automated alert creation, deduplication, and triage queues to prevent security analyst burnout.

4. **Seamless Gateway SDK & Signature Security**:
   - Integrates server-side Razorpay Test Mode order creation and HMAC-SHA256 signature verification, demonstrating understanding of payment security protocols.
